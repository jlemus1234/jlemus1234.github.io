abstract: >
  MIXR was a website created with the intent of being a multiplayer game where you guessed songs and their
  artists. It is a way for users to explore music with their friends.
description: >
  The MIXR website was a multiplayer game. Users hear a 30 second clip of an unknown song, and then are
  able to select from 4 multiple-choice options what they think the song is. Once the user has guessed,
  the correct answer is revealed allowing them to find out more about the song or artist. Another song is
  then loaded and the game continues. Both players are presented with multiple songs to guess, and the
  user with the most correct at the end of the session wins.


  My work was primarily in the backend, as well as in helping troubleshoot problems with front-end
  development. I worked on getting basic login features working, storing and retrieving scores as well as
  other user data, and hosting the website.
